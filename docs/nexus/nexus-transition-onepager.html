Coming to Pulse

Nexus is becoming your home base.

One screen that starts your day for you: who to reach, what's about to renew, which replies are waiting — ranked and ready, with one-click actions. Everything you use on the Home tab today is coming along. Nothing is being deleted, and nothing changes about your data.

What's new: “Your Day”

The top of Nexus becomes a short, ranked list that answers “what should I do next?” — assembled automatically from everything Pulse already knows. Each item says who, why, and gives you one button to act. Work it top to bottom and your day is handled.

Dr. Chen replied “Interested” — yesterday, still unanswered → Open reply
Sunrise Health renewal, $14k — 45 days out, no deal open → Open account
Call Sarah at Riverside — Day 8 of your campaign → Log the call

Not ready for something? Hit “Not today” and it politely returns tomorrow. Finish the real work (answer the reply, log the call) and the item clears itself — the list never nags about things you've already done.
