What the briefing does

The top of Nexus answers one question: what should I do next? It is assembled automatically from what Pulse already knows about your work. Each item says who, why, and gives you one button. Not ready for something? Hit Not today and it politely returns tomorrow. Finish the real work and the item clears itself. The list never nags about things you already did.

Not in sales? It is still built for you

The briefing shows whatever kinds of work you actually have. If you manage products, it leads with waiting requests and your due tasks. If you do assessment work, it leads with your tasks and deadlines. No deals means no deal rows. And if metrics are your first stop each morning, put the My Numbers widget right at the top of your widget area: pick your favorites from all 16 key metrics. The full metrics catalog lives in Reports, where the deep numbers already are.
